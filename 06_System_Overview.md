# Hospital Management System Overview

The Hospital Management System is designed to
organize information related to hospital operations.

It consists of different modules:

1. Patient Management:
   Maintains patient details and medical history.

2. Doctor Management:
   Stores doctor information and specialization.

3. Appointment Management:
   Maintains appointment dates, times, and status.

4. Medical Records:
   Stores diagnosis, symptoms, and treatment details.

5. Prescription Management:
   Maintains prescribed medicines and dosage.

6. Billing Management:
   Maintains consultation and other hospital charges.

7. Room Management:
   Maintains room details and availability.

The repository provides a structured knowledge
base for these modules.
