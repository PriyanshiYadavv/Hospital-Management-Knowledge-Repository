# Knowledge Facts

1. Every patient has a unique Patient ID.
2. Every doctor has a specific specialization.
3. A patient can book multiple appointments.
4. A doctor can handle multiple appointments.
5. Every medical record is associated with a patient.
6. A prescription contains medicine and dosage details.
7. A hospital room can be assigned to only one patient
   at a time.
8. A hospital bill may include consultation,
   medicine, and room charges.
