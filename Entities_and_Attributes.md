# Entities and Attributes

| Entity | Attributes |
|---|---|
| Patient | Patient ID, Name, Age, Gender, Contact |
| Doctor | Doctor ID, Name, Specialization, Availability |
| Appointment | Appointment ID, Date, Time, Status |
| Medical Record | Record ID, Diagnosis, Symptoms, Treatment |
| Prescription | Prescription ID, Medicine, Dosage, Duration |
| Department | Department ID, Department Name |
| Bill | Bill ID, Consultation Fee, Medicine Charges, Total |
| Hospital Room | Room ID, Room Type, Availability, Charges |
